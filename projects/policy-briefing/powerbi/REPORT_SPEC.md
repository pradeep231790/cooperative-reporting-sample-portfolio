# Report specification: Policy Briefing: Cooperative Services Sample

Build one report page per section. Apply `theme.json` (View > Themes > Browse for themes).

## Page: Sample scorecard

Illustrative position on cooperative-service rollout measures.

- **Card visuals (one per KPI)**: KPI cards: e-PACS achieved, ERP onboarding, Audit and YEP FY2024-25, Ticket resolution rate, Enhancements completed, PACS identified for reorganisation
- **Clustered bar chart (sorted descending)**: PACS covered by each milestone (of all PACS). Source: `statea_scorecard`
- **Line chart**: e-PACS progress as reported (% of PACS). Source: `statea_progress_timeline`
- **Table or matrix with conditional formatting**: Scorecard with sources. Source: `statea_scorecard`

## Page: Sample directions tracker

Generalized sample actions and their recorded evidence.

- **Card visuals (one per KPI)**: KPI cards: Directions tracked, Directions tracked, Next customization deadline
- **Table or matrix with conditional formatting**: Directions and State A evidence. Source: `policy_directions`

## Page: Integrations and enhancements

Portal integrations with their status, and the Concept Note enhancement requirements by theme and module.

- **Card visuals (one per KPI)**: KPI cards: Integrations requested, Enhancements completed, Concept Note requirements
- **100% stacked bar (one per group)**: Integration requests (4-Dec-2025). Source: `statea_scorecard`
- **100% stacked bar**: Enhancement requests (9-Feb-2026). Source: `statea_scorecard`
- **Clustered bar chart (sorted descending)**: Concept Note by theme. Source: `concept_note`
- **Clustered bar chart (sorted descending)**: Concept Note by module. Source: `concept_note`
- **Table or matrix with conditional formatting**: Portal integrations. Source: `integrations`
- **Table or matrix with conditional formatting**: Concept Note requirements. Source: `concept_note`

## Page: Region A and Region B

Comparable sample progress measures as a share of the anonymized population in scope.

- **Card visuals (one per KPI)**: KPI cards: State A PACS in scope, State B PACS in scope, Support centres State A / State B
- **100% stacked bar (one per group)**: Share of PACS reached (%). Source: `statea_vs_stateb`
- **Table or matrix with conditional formatting**: State A vs State B. Source: `statea_vs_stateb`

## Page: Milestones and deadlines

Review dates, letters and deadlines on one timeline, plus the daily and monthly records the e-PACS SOP requires.

- **Table or matrix with conditional formatting**: Timeline. Source: `milestones`
- **Text box**: Records required under the e-PACS SOP
- **Text box**: Online working rule
